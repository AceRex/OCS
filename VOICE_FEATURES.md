# OCS Voice Control: Complete Feature Guide

The OCS (Open Control System) now features a state-of-the-art, continuous background voice activation system specifically optimized for scripture projection and live presentations.

## 1. Core Architecture
- **Autonomous Background Monitoring:** The app listens continuously. No "Push-to-Talk" button is required.
- **Local AI Brain:** Uses an optimized **Whisper AI** model running entirely offline on your machine—no internet required for transcription.
- **Smart Efficiency (VAD):** Integrated Voice Activity Detection (VAD) monitors your volume. The AI only "wakes up" when it hears you talking, saving 90% of CPU power during silence.

## 2. Audio Engineering & Quality
- **Auto Noise Cancellation:** Built-in hardware suppression strips out background room noise and static.
- **High-Pass Rumble Filter:** A digital filter removes low-end "HVAC rumble" and wind noise (below 100Hz) to improve AI accuracy.
- **Auto Gain Control:** Automatically levels your voice so the AI can hear you clearly whether you are close to the mic or far away.

## 3. Voice Command Patterns (Natural Language)
The system understands how people actually talk. You don't need to be a robot.

### A. Scripture Lookups
**Trigger word:** Always start with **"Media"**.
- **Natural:** *"Media the book of John chapter three verse sixteen"*
- **Short:** *"Media Genesis one one"*
- **Casual:** *"Media please read the book of Psalms twenty three"*
- **Chapter Only:** *"Media John three"* (Defaults to verse 1)

### B. Sequential Navigation
Once a verse is showing, you can navigate without repeating the book:
- **Forward:** *"Media next verse"*, *"Next one"*, or simply *"Next"* (no "Media" required).
- **Backward:** *"Media previous verse"*, *"Go back"*, or simply *"Back"*.
- **Smart Wrapping:** If you are at the end of a chapter, saying "Next" will automatically jump to the next chapter.

### C. Context-Aware Jumping (Short-term Memory)
If you are already in a chapter (e.g., John 3), you can jump between verses instantly:
- *"Media go to verse twenty-six"*
- *"Media verse ten"*
- *"Media chapter four"* (jumps to the next chapter in the same book).

### D. Dynamic Highlighting
Draw attention to specific words on the big screen:
- **Command:** *"Media highlight [Word]"* or *"Media yellow [Word]"*.
- **Multi-Word:** *"Media highlight God and world"* (Highlights both words at once).
- **Phonetic Safety:** The AI understands misinterpretations like *"I like"* or *"I life"* as commands to highlight.
- **Marking:** You can also say *"Media mark [word]"*.

## 4. Stability & Diagnostics
- **Acoustic Autocorrect:** A "Fuzzy Logic" engine automatically corrects misheard Bible book names (e.g., "Jenna Case" -> "Genesis").
- **Visual Feedback (Bottom Bar):**
    - **RMS Meter:** See your microphone volume in real-time.
    - **VAD Indicator:** Turns **Green** when the AI is listening.
    - **Heard Log:** Shows exactly what the AI transcribed.
    - **Internal Log:** Shows step-by-step what the system is doing.
- **Emergency Sync:** A blue **Sync** button in the top bar allows for instant microphone and AI re-calibration.
