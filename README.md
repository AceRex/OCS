# OCS Desktop — Intelligent Church Presentation & Worship Workstation

**OCS (Organised Church Service)** is the next-generation sanctuary presentation workstation, engineered from the ground up to automate church visual media, voice-driven scripture projection, live broadcast overlays, and sermon archiving with unmatched offline-first reliability.

Built specifically for church technicians, volunteer media teams, and worship directors, OCS Desktop replaces complex, cluttered software with an intuitive, dynamic presentation engine that keeps the entire congregation engaged throughout every service.

## 🖥️ Flagship Capabilities

### 1. Voice-Driven Scripture Projection (AI Speech Recognition)
- **Zero-Typing Verse Projection**: OCS listens to the preacher's spoken words in real time and automatically finds, highlights, and displays the exact Bible chapter and verse on the sanctuary screens.
- **Support for Multi-Translations**: Instant multi-version rendering across KJV, NKJV, NIV, AMP, ESV, NLT, and regional dialects.
- **Pre-Caching & Offline Accuracy**: Embedded phonetic matching and local Bible indexes ensure instant, flawless scripture rendering even without internet connectivity.

### 2. Dual-Display Multi-Canvas Compositor
- **Main Congregation Projection**: High-definition, typography-rich rendering of hymns, dynamic motion backgrounds, scriptures, and sermon points.
- **Stage Foldback Display**: Dedicated confidence monitor for pastors and choir members showing upcoming lyrics, stage clocks, speaker countdowns, and private AV alerts.
- **NDI & Alpha-Key Broadcast Output**: Broadcast-grade lower-thirds and transparent graphics ready for OBS, vMix, and live streaming video switchers.

### 3. Automated Sermon Archiving & Multi-Track Recording
- **High-Fidelity Audio Capture**: Automatically captures and records service audio directly from your audio interface.
- **Automated Intro/Outro Bumper Merging**: Stitches church branded video/audio bumpers at the beginning and end of sermons automatically.
- **Instant Sermon Notes PDF Generation**: Automatically transcribes and exports formatted, printable sermon outlines and scripture citations for the congregation.

### 4. Service Agenda Planner & Automated Cues
- **Comprehensive Service Flow**: Plan worship sets, choir specials, sermon segments, and benedictions with exact time allocations.
- **Automated Lifecycle Media Cues**: Trigger intro chimes, background color transitions, warning cues, and automatic blackouts when sermon timers expire.

### 5. 100% Offline-First Architecture & 60-Day Free Trial
- **Sanctuary Stability**: Runs completely offline during Sunday services with local caching of all media, scriptures, and hymns.
- **60-Day Free Trial**: Full access to all workstation features with seamless cloud license sync when connected.
